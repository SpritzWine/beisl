Wordmark

Why these, and why not the wheel

Recommended · Das Glasl

The only mark where name, product, and picture are one sentence: the pub serves the wine, and the wine's contents are the layer verdict. It's cheerful — a good run looks like a good pour — which is the tool's own stance that confirming a good game is a first-class result, not a null one.

Declined · the Riesenrad

A Ferris-wheel frametime gauge is handsome, but the Riesenrad is Prater's icon in the family (Spritz · Prater · Beisl). Spending Vienna's most iconic silhouette on the pub-named sibling would strip-mine the family — and below 32px it collapses into the generic circular-gauge cliché. Declined for family reasons, not aesthetic ones.
